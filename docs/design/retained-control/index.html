These bounded C1 ownership and C3 drain models support DN-0056. The historical CP-0026 records the transition; the specification defines language semantics. The models do not. The C1 correspondence map states its premises, proved claims and reviewed product correspondence.

Module Scope
Ownership Finite owner inventories, transfer and restore transitions, completion, support, generations and failure accounting.
OwnershipExamples Concrete positive and negative traces over those transitions.
DrainSafety Finite focused-State normal returns, port cases and the checked Store-close predecessor.

The C1 candidate passed independent source review and a frozen scoped AD-0012 integration build. The gate record pins its sources, compiler, review and reports. C1's retained result was independently verified.

The C3 drain correspondence records the concrete control, value and completion premises used by the bounded analysis. Its reviewed source completed a separate frozen gate; its receipt passed independent audit. Linked validation uses the drain analysis; the connected C2/C3 checkpoint is retained and accepted.
