# Contribute to Ash

> Bring a concrete question, a small program, or a clear explanation. There are several ways to contribute.

Page: https://dikini.github.io/ash/contribute/  
Authority: website-guidance

Updated: 2026-10-18

Ash benefits from careful questions as much as implementation work. Begin with the current behavior, make the uncertainty concrete, and keep claims proportional to the evidence.

## Explore the language

Run the [tour](/ash/learn/tour/), try a small variation, and compare the result with the [specification](https://github.com/dikini/ash-lang/blob/bade38c635755a608695dd58c8cbef1254aace4c/spec/README.md). A minimal program and its exact command, compiler revision, output, and expected behavior make a useful report.

Use the [Ash repository](https://github.com/dikini/ash-lang) to find current contribution and issue entry points. Read its contribution guidance before changing language or compiler code.

## Research a language question

The [design index](https://github.com/dikini/ash-lang/blob/bade38c635755a608695dd58c8cbef1254aace4c/docs/design/README.md) and [development trajectory](https://github.com/dikini/ash-lang/blob/bade38c635755a608695dd58c8cbef1254aace4c/docs/trajectory.md) show current investigations and dependencies. Distinguish a research idea from accepted semantics. A prototype or passing example can supply evidence without establishing a new language guarantee.

## Improve learning material

Study lessons live in [ash-doc](https://github.com/dikini/ash-doc). Its contribution guidance governs prose, executable examples, evidence, and edition targets. Keep a lesson's explanation, canonical program, and checks aligned.

Website introductions and navigation belong to ash-site. We welcome clearer explanations, broken-link reports, and examples that help readers evaluate the language. Do not silently copy a study chapter into website content.

## Choose a bounded change

Before starting, identify the reader or programmer problem, the source of truth, and what would demonstrate an improvement. Follow the current upstream workflow rather than a historical article's instructions.

If you are just looking around, [language status](/ash/status/) and [Study Ash](/ash/study/) are good next stops.
