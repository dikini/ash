All design notes

This is implementation evidence for the bounded DN-0036 experiment. The user accepted the current completed cost measurements and authorized calibrated admission with explicitly reported deficits of at most 10%. It does not change current Ash semantics or establish production architecture.

Selected allowance

The ordinary modular public driver now selects separate cumulative allowances for source checking, final elaboration and independent Core validation. The largest completed measurements are rounded up to 65,536 charged units. Each rounded nominal budget gets at most another 10%, truncated to whole units. The margin's denominator is the nominal budget; rounding headroom is separate.

The earlier completed Core measurement 1,609,101 also fits the same allowance. The calibration input is pinned at 53780ef. Charged units include traversal, comparisons, records and reservations; they are neither executed instructions nor a conversion from CPU time or RSS. These bounds are a practical experimental admission policy for continued feature development, not a universal complexity bound or an optimization.

Only the public modular driver selects the larger allowances. Standalone Type work remains 327,680 and standalone Core work remains 983,040. Explicit caller budgets retain their exact limits. Resolution, linked-interface preparation, constraints, syntax, builders, rewrites, allocation admission, runtime and depth limits retain their existing policy. In particular, earlier linked-interface provider preparation has its own ledger; the final-elaborator measurement covers applying its checked result, lowering and final zonking.

Checking, preparation and independent validation run once, with no retry after exhaustion. Core helpers debit the selected validator's remaining allowance and charge their actual child work back to the parent on failure as well as success. Scope, kind, identity, evidence and native ownership checks remain required.

Explicit reporting

Observation.compilation_work() returns the source, final-elaborator and independent-Core measurements. Each PhaseCost contains its owning phase, charged used count and completed flag. Its assessment() method derives a WorkAssessment variant without duplicating the charged count:

  • WithinBudget;
  • AcceptedDeficit { units }, an explicit future optimization target;
  • OutsideAllowance { units }.

phase.budget() and phase.hard_limit() expose the denominators and stopping limits. optimization_target() identifies accepted deficits. A successful public run completes all three observed phases. A cost report grants no checking or Core authority.

FileFailure.compilation_work() retains completed prior phases and measured partial failing phases without replacing the original diagnostic. Absent fields mean unobserved. A partial charged count does not estimate all work needed to finish: the next refused bulk reservation may exceed the remaining allowance even when the reported completed charges fit the nominal budget. Input loading failures acquire no fabricated compiler measurements.

CPU/RSS assessment remains separate and offline. The adopted physical assessment accepts measured excesses up to 10% against each original calibrated review threshold and current-workload target. It records the baseline, observed cost, absolute and relative deficit, and optimization target. A larger excess requires review; missing or out-of-range measurements remain outside calibration. Its 52 primary measured cases need no accepted deficit. The separately retained structural cross-check has nine accepted and five outside-calibration cases; nine IDs overlap the primary set. This is recalibration on examined data, not unseen-program validation or a fresh CPU/RSS measurement of this change.

Execution evidence and boundary

The four original expanded SQLite assertions pass through the public driver: copying Unicode/bytes/nulls/empty values, rejected bound NUL, persistence with statement reuse and reopening, and captured-control resume without reacquisition. The complete connected batch passes 69 integration tests, including native arena lifecycle, retention, rows, source control and reporting. The focused 18 driver tests pass, including independent Core failure propagation and exact cumulative parent-prefix admission. The exact nominal/margin/first-refused unit policy boundary passes separately. After the final report representation refinement, all 40 focused tests pass again. Commands, pins and results are in the evidence index; the contract review records its findings and resolution.

The larger SQLite library, native adapter and test inputs are preserved independent work. They were used unchanged as regression inputs in Common; this shared delivery does not silently commit them. Its input and executable source hashes, commands and logs are recorded in the adjacent evidence index. SQLite's preserving delivery and example-host reporting follow the verified shared integration. Arena scalar allocation and proof/Core alignment retain their separate scope and status.
