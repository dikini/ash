Ash-doc is the study companion to this website. It gives explanations space to develop through examples, exercises, and checked observations. It remains a separate project with its own compiler target and publication cycle.

**Publication status: unpublished.** The links below open a reviewed source snapshot on GitHub. There is no published HTML edition, PDF, or EPUB download offered by this site yet.

[Read Learn Ash](https://github.com/dikini/ash-doc/tree/dc9c495563aafac1f37891285f723c99a3fd64a6/content/learn/README.md) for the sequential introduction. It begins with one program and continues through functions, modules, data, recursion, and abstraction.

Follow [the study edition's setup](https://github.com/dikini/ash-doc/tree/dc9c495563aafac1f37891285f723c99a3fd64a6/README.md#prepare-ash). Its material targets **diag-ash** at Ash revision **9ec5920**, not the website tour's newer checkpoint. Read the [edition README](https://github.com/dikini/ash-doc/tree/dc9c495563aafac1f37891285f723c99a3fd64a6/README.md) for its exact verification and publication state.
