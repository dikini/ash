All five baseline programs completed their original native assertions. Three candidate programs completed with the temporary 20,971,520-unit diagnostic ceiling. Copying and bound NUL exhausted that ceiling; both failed attempts are retained. A bounded continuation changed only the diagnostic ceiling to 268,435,456 and added refusal-counter observation. Both programs then completed the original native assertions. All five candidate results therefore have completed measurements; these diagnostic passes do not establish admission under normal limits.

The first comparison returned after 156.955 seconds, the continuation after 60.661 seconds. Each used one Cargo job and reused compiled artifacts between programs. Exact Common, SQLite and Original source/index/HEAD guards were restored after each round. Normal policies are restored and no Cargo, Rust, LSP or Lean engines remain. No candidate source or diagnostic ceiling is selected for integration. Full architecture and workspace verification were not repeated for this unsuccessful candidate.

The real copying program published 127 Raw and 120 Evidence preparations and reused the root at six further occurrences. Forwarding published seven Raw and four Evidence preparations and reused the root twice. These counts establish that the connected path executed; they do not explain the work amplification or prove which sharing would be valid. Broad merging of environments by equal types would discard live route, readiness, capture and creation distinctions.

On resume, first attribute the additional work before another implementation round. A read-only review found an unread local_memo and duplicate journal in the candidate's producer.rs; consuming local values into the sole completed memo may avoid actual cloning. Preserve ordered alternatives, saved routes, authority and duplicate-publication checks. This is an investigation candidate, not an implemented fix or an explanation of the 27.7-fold copying regression. Core alignment remains paused.

Retained evidence and reproduction

The manifest authenticates the capsule's raw receipts, original runner sources and patches. Large full-worktree snapshots are losslessly compressed; the archive index records their decoded names and SHA-256 hashes. Logs use .txt here and retain their original bytes. Failed normal and 20,971,520-unit attempts remain separate from completed runs.

The recorded runners include local evidence paths and are historical launch records. To reproduce the programs from Git in an isolated worktree:

  1. Start at Common commit 5a1da1ce97808ba68ad961a5cc6d80dadb6d09de.
  2. Apply measurement-inputs.patch, then native-forwarding-overlay.patch.
  3. For the baseline, apply diagnostic-forward.patch.
  4. For the candidate, apply candidate-forward.patch and candidate-connected-coverage.patch before diagnostic-forward.patch. For completed copying/NUL costs, replace the temporary 20_971_520 ceiling in src/types.rs with 268_435_456.
  5. Run the exact commands in the comparison results and the copying/NUL continuation with CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=1, no ASH_* overrides and one test thread.

Use independent baseline and candidate worktrees, or reverse only these exact patches between lanes. These are disposable diagnostic source changes. Keep them out of source delivery commits and restore normal ceilings afterward. The public native assertions are unchanged by the diagnostic patches.
