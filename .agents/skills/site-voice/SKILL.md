# Site voice

The Ash site states; it does not sell. **REQUIRED:** read `docs/site-voice.md`
before drafting or revising any site copy. The hard rules, compressed:

1. Plain nouns in headlines; no promotional adjectives, no campaigns.
2. One proof sentence under every claim; never two abstract sentences in a
   row.
3. Strong verbs ("must keep it", not "has to fulfil"); cut verbs that carry
   no weight.
4. One level of jargon per surface; one name per destination on a surface.
5. The contract vocabulary — declare, contract, keep, check, honest — is the
   shared metaphor family.
6. Honesty in the positive ("not promised", not "should be expected").
7. The accurate word over the familiar one; a word must already exist in the
   site's documents.
8. Rhythm and repetition are deliberate; two emphasised texts never share a
   key word.

Scope: website content pages only. The specification and docs publications
follow the source writing guide; the study book follows
`writing-ash-documentation`. Do not apply this voice there.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Counter |
| --- | --- |
| Rhetorical opener ("Found a bug or have an idea?") | State what the page offers |
| Imperative beckoning ("Step inside the work") | Name the content; the link is the invitation |
| Promotional filler ("helps shape Ash", "as development unfolds") | One concrete fact instead |
| Passive hedge ("breaking changes should be expected") | "Stability and production readiness are not promised" |
