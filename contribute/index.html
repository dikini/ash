Contribute to Ash

Bring a concrete question, a small program, or a clear explanation. There are several ways to contribute.

Reviewed 18 October 2026

Ash benefits from careful questions as much as implementation work. Begin with the current behavior, make the uncertainty concrete, and keep claims proportional to the evidence.

Explore the language

Run the tour, try a small variation, and compare the result with the specification. A minimal program and its exact command, compiler revision, output, and expected behavior make a useful report.

Use the Ash repository to find current contribution and issue entry points. Read its contribution guidance before changing language or compiler code.

Research a language question

The design index and development trajectory show current investigations and dependencies. Distinguish a research idea from accepted semantics. A prototype or passing example can supply evidence without establishing a new language guarantee.

Improve learning material

Study lessons live in ash-doc. Its contribution guidance governs prose, executable examples, evidence, and edition targets. Keep a lesson’s explanation, canonical program, and checks aligned.

Website introductions and navigation belong to ash-site. We welcome clearer explanations, broken-link reports, and examples that help readers evaluate the language. Do not silently copy a study chapter into website content.

Choose a bounded change

Before starting, identify the reader or programmer problem, the source of truth, and what would demonstrate an improvement. Follow the current upstream workflow rather than a historical article’s instructions.

If you are just looking around, language status and Study Ash are good next stops.
