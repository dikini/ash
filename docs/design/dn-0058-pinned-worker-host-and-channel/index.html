Accepted and implemented

The fixed two-worker host and copied FIFO byte channels are accepted and implemented on main as part of ffi-ash. AD-0017 records the architecture. The static and dynamic specifications define the language contract.

Accepted design

Each worker remains on its carrier thread and initializes its own Ash root. CPU affinity is not required. Store slots and continuations remain local to their worker. The enclosing network owner retains both carriers and channel backing through settlement.

Worker entries are closed named top-level functions. Admission checks their linked definitions, effects, dependencies and captures. Startup inputs are copied. A caller-owned closure, Store, cell, region or provider is not transferred.

Send copies finite Bytes into channel-owned storage before acceptance. Receive reserves the FIFO head and constructs the receiver's complete result in its selected region before removing the message. A failed construction restores the head. Application and Control envelopes share each direction's FIFO.

The channel authenticates message kind and sending side. Ordinary Ash code can call send_control; the compiler does not require a special protocol driver or certify acknowledgements, ledger invariants or per-message processing. The ordinary protocol owns those checks and error paths.

finish_send seals the sending polarity. End follows seal and the accepted queue's exhaustion. finish_receive requires the actual incoming channel to be sealed and empty with no outstanding transport operation. Transport End does not establish application processing or cleanup completion.

Watch registration rechecks readiness atomically with registration. Readable includes message, End or failure; writable includes send capacity, Closed or failure. Wakes identify generation-checked registrations and require a new state check. Store restoration remains local to its owning executor.

Returned workers retain their roots, evaluators and bindings on their carriers. After observing both normal returns, finished endpoints, completed operations and removed registrations, the coordinator authorizes carrier-local teardown. Settlement then joins the carriers and releases backing after that teardown completes.

Why this choice

Copied bytes give each receiver independently owned storage. They require no region transfer, shared ordinary heap or continuation migration. A mutex protects transport state; ordinary Store operations remain local.

Proof and verification obligations

Public worker tests, transport tests and carrier tests cover copied delivery, bounded FIFO state, readiness, failure and settlement. Scheduling tests exercise worker-local control policies. The library guide records ordinary protocol execution and the generated-image limitation.

The worker/channel model, ledger model and one-way model retain historical conditional proof results. The earlier protocol-specific checker requirements were superseded on 2026-10-31 and are retired.

Terminal failure holder

After terminal failure prevents checked local completion, the affected carrier retains its interrupted evaluator, heap, frames, bindings and outstanding support for the remaining process lifetime. It reports incomplete teardown. The holder executes no Ash, retries no work and moves no continuation to the coordinator. The host marks transport terminal and retains its support.

Not implemented

Worker migration, region ownership transfer, shared ordinary graphs, shared Stores, worker recovery and Network private-image encoding are not implemented. The implementation supplies no fairness, deadlock-freedom or termination guarantee.
